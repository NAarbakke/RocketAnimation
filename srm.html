Model: the grain is voxelised and every point gets its distance to the gas-exposed surface (3-D Euclidean distance transform). The surface regresses at one rate everywhere, so that distance is the web burned when the flame arrives. Burning area Ab(w) = dV/dw. Chamber pressure is quasi-steady, from mass generated = mass through the choked throat: Pc = (a·ρ·c*·Kn)^(1/(1−n)). Burn rate r = a·Pcⁿ with temperature sensitivity σp. Thrust = CF·Pc·Aₜ from ideal nozzle flow. Not modelled: erosive burning, throat erosion, two-phase losses and the ignition transient (the ramp is cosmetic).
